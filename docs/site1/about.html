About

Mission, commitment, and 30+ years of field experience

Mission

Jon's goal is to provide cost-effective project management, owner's representation, and professional support services customized to meet each customer's specialized needs and requirements — while upholding the highest quality and integrity.

Commitment

Outperforming industry standards and exceeding client expectations will always be Jon's commitment to customers and to the work he enjoys.

Background

For over 30 years, Jon has provided project management and owner's representation for key, diverse end-user project types, including:

  • Non-profit capital improvement programs
  • Renovation work
  • Facility assessments
  • Public works and school improvements
  • Land development and infrastructure
  • Class A office buildings
  • Auto malls
  • Retail development
  • Industrial parks
  • Recreational and amusement projects
  • Themed environments
  • Golf course development
  • Multi-family housing, subdivisions, custom homes, and luxury production housing

Project Role & Responsibilities

Jon provides the skill and expertise to intertwine all the elements and disciplines of a successful project. From pro forma stage through furniture, fixture and equipment fit-out, close-out, final job-cost summarization, and post-completion, Jon provides owners' representation to ensure adherence to budgets and schedules — recommending strategies, project delivery methods, and coordinating agency processing.

Summary Experience

Extensive project management, forward planning, purchasing, job cost control, and field experience across public sector and non-profit capital improvements, recreation projects, Class A office buildings, industrial parks, retail space, land development, golf courses, and over 4,000 residential units. Jon also provides testimony, litigation support, and forensic job-cost review when required.

Education

Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, with an emphasis in Finance and a minor in Urban and Transportation Geography — San Diego State University.
