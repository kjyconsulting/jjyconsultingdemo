About This Archive

Why this page exists

A record, not a storefront

Jonathan J. Young Consulting was an active project management and owner's representation practice serving Nevada and California for more than 25 years. Jon has since retired, and the practice is no longer accepting new engagements or client inquiries.

This site is maintained solely as a professional retrospective — a record of the kind of work the practice did and the standards it held itself to. It intentionally does not include a contact form, phone number, or active email, since there is no one monitoring this page on the practice's behalf.

If you are researching a past project or need historical records for an unrelated purpose, those records would need to be sought through the original project's other parties of record (architect, general contractor, lender, or local permitting agency), as this archive does not maintain project files.

Thank you for reading about the work.
